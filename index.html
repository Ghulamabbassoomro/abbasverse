Live Performance · HDA

Things I Want to DoOne Day

Performer · Concept 30 June 2025 IMC Room, HDA Darmstadt

A durational performance exploring time, dreams deferred, and the quiet weight of our own postponements. I performed wearing a black shalwar kameez and ajrak — my Sindhi cultural identity made visible. Post-it notes attached to my body carried dreams I have told myself to pursue "one day." A two-sided mirror: one face for me, one for the audience. Visitors were invited to write their own deferred dreams and place them on my body.

The shalwar kameez represents where I come from. The ajrak carries centuries of Sindhi tradition, identity, and resistance. The performance was an act of shared vulnerability — inviting strangers into the weight of possibility.

3D Audio · Fulldome · Jena Festival

Echoes of a ThoughtThat Died

Title & Exposé — Ghulam Abbas 3D Audio Composition Fulldome Interactive

An immersive 3D audio piece pulling the listener into the mental disintegration of an unnamed subject — someone losing not their life, but their inner continuity. Inspired by Poe's The Fall of the House of Usher, the work unfolds across five emotional zones: Dissociation, Panic, Longing, Fragmentation, and Collapse.

I wrote the title and the full exposé for this work, and contributed to the 3D audio composition. The piece was part of the IMC MediaArtCulture Summer Exhibition at Dieburg Museum as a Fulldome Touch Design interactive piece — and has since been nominated and selected for the Jena Full Dome Festival.

Writer?

— Abbas Writes —

Whenever I try to capture something, I can't help but capture loneliness, detachment, looking from the outside, or isolation. Maybe it's just reflecting my phase in life, but who to blame? The people with whom you invested so much of your energy just to be with them, or just life? Where everyone is hanging around the other with an agenda, that this would lead me to this, which is also fine and the way this world works. But what happened to genuine connections? Or are those ones just made in school? Or maybe I am looking at it wrong, or doing something wrong? Whenever I try to capture something, somehow I end up capturing distance.

Bench — Abbas Writes —

The idea of having a bench outside, in a park, or in a balcony or roof is such a soothing one because it has a different comfort. You can't sit on it for long enough but the momentarily comfort does trump a lot of comfortable chairs. You automatically start talking about life on it, big issues, small issues, the idea of having tea sounds so intriguing or just taking it in the outdoor air in its truest form especially in winters. But perhaps I am not talking about bench at all. People are like that too. You find these amazing people like a breath of fresh air for a short time in your life perhaps whom you share everything with, the connections perhaps doesn't hold for long as "Life happens" but still the conversations the butterflies in stomach of having a new friend with similar interests in shows, movies, books and so on becomes so intriguing that you just can't help it but enjoy it or sometimes mess up too. Here is to my bench people making an impact in life but with whom perhaps the connection is not the same and to all the new ones making the life exciting.
